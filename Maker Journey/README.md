# Team Lab Photos

Real lab photos supplied by the team, received 2026-10-26.
Photo capture dates are not recorded in the files — EXIF is empty, and
file dates are receipt dates, not capture dates. No capture date is
stated for these photos in this document.

## What each photo shows
- 01-lab-laptop-work.jpg: team member at a lab bench with a laptop, a
  wired white prototype unit on the bench behind, blue pegboard with tools.
  No ID badge clearly visible in this frame. Laptop screen content is not
  legible enough to identify as a specific V-ORRA screen.
- 02-lab-soldering.jpg: team member soldering at a lab bench, laptop with
  code, oscilloscope on the right, blue pegboard, ladder and safety poster
  in the background. A blue lanyard with an ID card is in frame at the chest,
  but the ID text is not legible at this resolution.
- 03-lab-coding-multimeters.jpg: hands typing, laptop with code, MASTECH
  multimeter boxes (MAS830L / MS8217) on the bench, blue pegboard.
  Rotated frame. No ID badge visible. Face only partly in frame at the edge.
- 04-lab-coding-back-view.jpg: team member from behind, coding on a laptop,
  oscilloscope on the right, box with wires/components, blue pegboard with
  screwdriver sets, scissors, pliers. No ID badge visible.
- 05-vehicle-demo-bench-prototype.jpg: assembling the INITIAL vehicle
  module, on a wooden bench, 1216 x 676, 229 KB, no EXIF date. Team
  description, confirmed 2026-10-26: "assembling initial vehicle module".
  Visible: white controller board with USB (top centre, rainbow Dupont
  wires), white breadboard, blue GPS board with ceramic antenna (lower
  centre), blue 2-channel relay module with red LEDs lit (right centre,
  on a brown perfboard), black 3-cell battery holder (top right), and an
  automotive headlamp bulb with red/black wires (far right). A hand is in
  frame at left. This is the INITIAL module at bench assembly stage, NOT
  the refined vehicle schematic as drawn — no SN74HC74 latch, no CD74HC423
  deadman, no 4PDT override switch, no Pololu buck are visible, and the
  relay here is a 2-channel module, not the single G5LE-1 NC relay in the
  refined schematic. The caption for this photo is "initial vehicle module,
  bench assembly", not the refined schematic built, a soldered final unit,
  a closed-course installed unit, or a road test. No enclosure, loose
  wires, exposed bulb and battery pack are visible.

and laptop — consistent, real phone photos, 589 x 1280, 55–68 KB each.
