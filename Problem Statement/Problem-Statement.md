# Problem Statement

Road users in Indian cities face two linked, everyday problems that
existing vehicle systems do not solve together:

1. Horn noise in silence / No Horn Zones, especially near hospitals,
   schools, and residential areas, where horn use continues even when
   a zone rule is in force.
2. High-beam glare in Low Beam Zones, where a driver may not notice
   a zone change in time, or may forget to dip the beam.

Target beneficiaries: drivers of equipped vehicles, residents and
patients near silence zones, and other road users affected by glare.
The system in this repo is for equipped vehicles on a surveyed,
closed-course demo first. It is not a deployed road enforcement system,
and it does not claim to control unequipped vehicles.

Note on numbers, as in the submitted deck: India recorded 177,175 road
fatalities in 2024, as cited in the deck. High-beam deaths are not
separately quantified nationally, and the deck discloses that gap.
No accident number in this repo is presented as a V-ORRA measurement.
