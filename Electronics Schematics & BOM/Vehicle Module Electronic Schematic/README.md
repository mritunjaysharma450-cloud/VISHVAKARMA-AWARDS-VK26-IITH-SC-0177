A dedicated 12 V demo pack passes through F1 (rating OPEN) to the actuator rail and Pololu D24V22F5 5 V buck. The ESP32-S3 DevKitC-1 VIN takes 5 V; its onboard regulator provides 3.3 V to the bare MaUWB, GPS and logic ICs. All demo returns share GND. There is no copper connection to the actual vehicle horn, lamp or battery. The logic/actuator branches are not galvanically isolated. USB power is disconnected when VIN is supplied.

UART0 IO43/44 is reserved for programming. MaUWB 3.3 V TTL uses UART1: IO17 TX -> tag RX, tag TX -> IO18 RX at 115200 baud. GPS uses UART2: IO4 TX -> GPS RX, GPS TX -> IO5 RX at nominal 9600 baud. IO4/5 are non-strapping pins chosen for digital UART use; IO0/3/45/46 strapping pins, ADC2 analogue use during Wi-Fi, and memory IO35–37 are avoided. MaUWB RESET/WAKEUP is OPEN until the exact module revision is matched; GPS PPS is intentionally unused.

IO7 is lamp-mode data and IO11 is the update strobe to SN74HC74 U5. The Q and complementary Q outputs drive separate HIGH and LOW demo lamps. TPS3839G33 U6 holds the latch clear during logic-rail start-up; the initial mode is LOW. No update strobe means the stored mode remains while the 3.3 V logic rail is alive, including an ESP-only reset. If logic power fails, retention is lost; neither lamp can stay on without demo actuator power. S2A removes the automatic MOSFET source return during override, then powers the common terminal of S4. S4 selects the manual HIGH or LOW return.

IO8 commands the G5LE-1 DC12 relay coil through Q2. IO10 supplies edges to the CD74HC423 U7 retriggerable monostable only while valid commands are fresh; U7 Q drives series MOSFET Q4. A stuck high or low IO10 has no new edge, so coil current ceases after the hardware pulse expires. R8=100 k and C3=4.7 uF set a preliminary pulse width; the 3.3 V and tolerance extremes require measurement before any timeout guarantee. The HC423 variant avoids the HC123 reset-release trigger. D1 suppresses coil flyback. The relay NC contact makes the demo horn available without coil power. S2B cuts coil supply in override; S2C grounds the horn return around K1, including a welded-open contact. S2D takes OVERRIDE_N low for IO9 logging. S2 is one mechanically ganged, break-before-make 4PDT functional MOCK; its exact purchased model, physical terminal map and contact current ratings are OPEN. S4 manual SPDT terminal map/rating is also OPEN.
