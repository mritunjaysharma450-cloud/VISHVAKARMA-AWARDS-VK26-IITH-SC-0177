# BOM Note — Electronics Schematics & BOM

Submitted deck BOM, as in the deck: pole ₹42,656.59 + vehicle ₹10,037.55 = ₹52,693.13 total, with a note that lab-available parts bring it to about ₹30,000–₹35,000. Prices in the deck are ex-shipping, tax, and customs, as stated there, and procurement lead time in the deck is stated both as 7 and as 7–10 business days — that inconsistency is in the deck, and is not resolved here by inventing a figure.

Revised schematics in this folder add parts that are not in that deck BOM: SN74HC74 lamp latch, TPS3839 supervisor, CD74HC423 horn deadman, Pololu buck, 4PDT override switch, SPDT manual switch, and a 12 V demo pack (deck vehicle power is described as a 10,000 mAh bank). Those additions are post-deck design fixes for lamp FREEZE and horn deadman, made 26 October 2026 due to component changes.

A Stage 3 BOM update lists those added parts with quantities and prices, and reconciles vehicle power (12 V pack vs 10,000 mAh bank) before any purchase. The added parts were not in the deck BOM, and the deck total is not the total for the revised schematics until that update is made.
